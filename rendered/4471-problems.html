The 5 shift reports are also counted under the cause they are filed by, as the desk counts them.

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant on file writes the day before the month. The shared parser reads every date month first, so 7 April becomes 4 July. It reaches every tenant through every channel: app, email and form. Fixed in engineering’s change “fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales”.

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type the month first

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint tells them, so their dates come out flipped. It only reaches tenants on booking-v3, and only through the form. The reports look the same as the parser’s (day and month swapped), but the fix is in the form’s hint, not the parser. Won’t fix: engineering retired booking-v3, so the hint is gone with it.

Intake cannot tell the two apart. It tags from the reporter’s browser, so it tags hint for an en-US browser and parser otherwise. The tenant decides the cause, not the browser.

shift: the date moves by one day

These reports are not flipped dates. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May. A meeting in Lisbon lands on Tuesday and the same meeting in Toronto on Monday. An invite arrives before its booking. This points to a time zone being dropped or applied twice. Still open: no change so far covers it. The desk read these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but still filed them under the parser by tenant, and the desk counts them there too.
